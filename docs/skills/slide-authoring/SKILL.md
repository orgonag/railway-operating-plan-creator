# Slide authoring maintenance
Use when changing the storyboard workflow. Read AGENTS.md first.
Keep geometry and document operations in model.ts, DOM interactions in editor.ts. Preserve one movement per slide and partial-cut selection. Exercise a ten-car cut with five selected, a locomotive plus cars, independent cuts on multiple tracks, undo/reload, manual routing, and presentation. Never infer coupling or operating validity. Changes to an earlier slide must not silently invalidate later animation starts.

For export changes, test real downloaded PDF/PPTX/PNG/SVG/ZIP files, HTML autoplay, and video decoding in Chromium. Only pause length is user-editable; animation duration stays automatic. Verify zero pauses, old files without pauses, apply-to-all, manual stopping, and straight tracks near diagonal junctions. Export titles and all instruction lines; escape authored text. Keep all video stream cleanup paths, including cancel and hidden-tab failures.

For canvas changes, verify click-click and drag drawing, endpoint continuation, duplicate/crossing rejection, all four turnout previews, grouped rotation/deletion and Undo. Legacy ungrouped tracks must still import. Check drawing and selection after zoom and pan, Page/Fit recovery, and ensure exports remain page-sized. Walk through the first-visit tutorial, dismiss/reload, and New replay; Help must work with existing plans without editing them.

Annotation checks: add/select/edit/move/delete text, arrows and end markers; resize arrow endpoints; use zoom and a narrow viewport. Verify labels overlapping tracks remain selectable, text double-click does not swallow drags, typing Delete does not delete the selection, and earlier-slide notes remain editable without unlocking geometry. Exercise save/open and local versus shared annotation ownership.

For endless-canvas changes, draw at negative coordinates and beyond the former 9600x5600 bounds. Check ordinary wheel pan, Shift-wheel horizontal pan, Ctrl/Meta-wheel zoom, Space-drag, Fit and Whole plan. Select multiple tracks by enclosing box, Shift-click and Ctrl/Meta+A; delete/undo and preserve complete turnout groups. Existing coordinates must not shift. Exports use drawingBounds across all slides and animation frames, including negative coordinates, while the editor grid covers its current view. SVG/HTML retain full geometry; raster width caps at 4800.

For movement changes, move a locomotive and multiple cars beyond a track endpoint, add a slide and drag an off-track car as anchor, then reverse on another slide. Verify initial animation positions and spacing, save/reload and HTML playback. Track suggestions must never constrain authored positions. Motion.translate preserves the entire selection when track-following would distort it; manual paths can include off-track waypoints.

Performance check: scene must reuse supplied bounds during paint, thumbnails, standalone export and video. Do not sample every movement in the plan per animation frame. Include save/open coordinates beyond 100000 in endless-canvas checks.

Reliability checks: type titles/instructions and press Escape before blur, then reload; click another step directly from a focused input. Exercise two simultaneous browser tabs: stale writes must fail visibly and preserve a downloadable draft after tab close. Test corrupt saved bytes and quota failure without overwriting originals or older session drafts. Never delete another page's recovery key after restoring a cached copy. Autosave checks must wait for completion, not read localStorage immediately after pointerup.

Revision checks: revise the initial snapshot without deleting later steps; preserve notes and end snapshots, flag movements, block export/full playback until ordered review. Rebuild a movement and preview it while later reviews remain. Remove referenced equipment and drag its stale representation: give a recovery message, no page error. Save/reload review flags and Undo. Frame imported and loaded content, enter presentation from a zoomed view, and restore that view on exit. Test a 50-step/100-car plan for responsiveness and actual HTML export. Keep authoring and import count boundaries consistent.

Track movement checks: select a line, drag its middle, join a nearby endpoint, and verify exact connectivity. Box-select/Shift-select multiple pieces and translate without changing their relative geometry. Turnouts move as groups. Try an overlapping destination and ensure no partial movement persists; Undo and reload. Repeat after zoom; verify green connection feedback, endpoint priority over grid rounding, and Alt bypass. Keep Track mode for drawing and Select/V for moving; L returns to drawing.

Regression checks: drag a cut by its middle car to an off-track destination and verify all selected cars retain spacing/orientation, including at animation endpoints. Export a multi-step PDF with FAST PNG compression and inspect file size; never ship uncompressed RGB pages.
