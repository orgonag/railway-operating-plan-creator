Verify zero pauses, old files without pauses, apply-to-all, manual stopping, and straight tracks near diagonal junctions. Export titles and all instruction lines; escape authored text. For movement changes, move a locomotive and multiple cars beyond a track endpoint, add a slide and drag an off-track car as anchor, then reverse on another slide. Exercise two simultaneous browser tabs: stale writes must fail visibly and preserve a downloadable draft after tab close.
