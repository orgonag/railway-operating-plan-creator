# Default car and locomotive lengths for side-view drawing

Researched 2026-10-20 by web search only. LOC = length over couplers (pulling faces). LOS = length over strikers (end of car body at the striker plate; couplers stick out about 2.5 to 3 ft beyond each end, so LOC is about 5 to 6 ft more than LOS). Builder sheets say all dimensions are approximate. Recommendation: use LOC as the one default for everything, because it is what sets spacing in a train.

Verified = figure read from a builder, lessor or reference page. Estimate = not found, derived from nearby cars.

## Side-view silhouettes for the illustrator

- 50 ft boxcar: plain rectangle, flat roof, single sliding door about one third in from the right on one side, small end-of-car ladders, shallow underframe, two 2-axle trucks well in from the ends.
- 60 ft high cube boxcar: same as above but longer and about 1.5 ft taller, usually a pair of doors or a wide 12 ft door, flat roof.
- Small-cube covered hopper (3,281): short, boxy body with vertical side stiffeners, two or three small hatch bumps on a flat roof, two to three sloped hopper bottoms visible, small roof walkway.
- Large-cube covered hopper (4,750, 5,161, 5,800): long, with a gently curved or slightly bulged side, three to four sloped bays under the body with an angled sill, roof ridge with round hatches, a catwalk and an end ladder.
- Open-top coal hopper: top is open and slightly heaped, side sheets with vertical ribs, body tapers to a flat bottom at the underframe, three to four hopper bays with sloped belly. For rotary coal gondola: straight vertical sides, flat bottom, round rotary couplers at one end.
- Mill gondola (52 ft): open-top rectangle, straight vertical slab sides with corrugated or ribbed panels, low-slung, ends slightly higher than sides; 66 ft version is the same, longer.
- General service tank car (30,000 gal): long horizontal cylinder on a thin underframe, one dome or manway platform on top with a ladder at each end, rounded heads, trucks near the ends.
- Pressure LPG tank car: shorter-diameter-looking, fatter cylinder (about 10 ft across), no dome, a protective housing on top with a platform, often a jacket or insulation; may have a thick head shield at the ends.
- Bulkhead flatcar: flat deck with a tall vertical bulkhead panel at each end, no sides or roof.
- Centerbeam flatcar: flat deck, tall bulkheads at the ends joined by a central vertical beam along the roof line (a flat truss shape down the length, triangular in end view).
- 89 ft flatcar: very long, flat deck, thin side sill, trucks set far in from the ends; often a trailer or container hitch at one end.
- Autorack: very tall, enclosed boxy slab with slotted louvres and thin horizontal ribs, flat roof, tall relative to length, no doors in the side.
- Double-stack well car: low, deep well slung between the trucks (lower than the deck at the ends), stacked on two levels of containers; the well sits low and the end structures are short.
- Reefer (72 ft mechanical): long smooth insulated box like a boxcar, a diesel unit housing at one end (taller bump on the end of the roof) and a vent on the sidewall.
- SD70ACe / ES44AC: long, low long hood with three-quarter-height cab at one end, large radiator wings at the rear; ES44AC has a more squared-off cab roof and a taller center cooling section. Three axles per truck (six axles), distinct fuel tank below.
- GP38-2: short hood with a high short hood at the front, a low long hood at the rear, a boxy cab, four axles (two 2-axle trucks).
- SW1500: very short, low end-cab switcher, one small narrow cab, a short hood behind it, four axles.

## Not verified or conflicting

- 4,750 and 5,800 cu ft covered hoppers: no builder sheet found. Figures are estimates.
- Open-top coal hopper: only a secondary page for a 53'1" body length. LOC is my estimate.
- 65 ft gondola: only a 66 ft and 62 ft figure found, not 65 ft.
- 30,000 gal general service tank: neighbours only.
- LPG tank: builders differ by 3 ft.
- Centerbeam: sources conflict. Check a 73 ft centerbeam from a Union Pacific, BNSF or Trinity spec before finalizing.
- Bulkhead and centerbeam FCA sheet URLs returned 404 on direct fetch (figures came from search results).
- SD70ACe and ES44AC lengths are published overall lengths, with no source naming the exact basis.
- No railroad equipment guide (UP, BNSF, CN, CPKC) car page was reached in this pass; only the CN flatcar sheet was found.
