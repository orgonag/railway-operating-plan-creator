# Railway Operating Plan Creator

I wanted a simple way to draw a yard and show how the railway would service it, step by step. That's what this is: a free tool for making visual operating plans.

Draw the tracks, add your locomotive and cars, then move them one step at a time. Add notes and arrows where you need them. It works in your browser—no account or installation.

**[Try it here](https://orgonag.github.io/railway-operating-plan-creator/)**

## How it works

1. **Draw the yard.** Add straight tracks, turnouts and labels. Select a piece to move or delete it.
2. **Set the starting position.** Drag in the locomotive, loaded cars and empties.
3. **Show the moves.** Click **+ Next step**, select the cars and drag them where they need to go. Adjust the suggested path if needed. One movement per step; reversing goes on the next step.

![Draw your track layout.](docs/images/01-draw-tracks.png)

![Move the cars and explain what happens.](docs/images/03-create-movement.png)

Click **Preview step** to check a move or **Play plan** to watch the whole thing. Set a pause so people have time to read your notes.

**Download** keeps an editable copy. **Export** gives you PDF, PowerPoint, images, video or an animated HTML file you can share.

Need a hand? Click **Help** in the app or read the [user guide](docs/user-guide.md).

## A quick disclaimer

This is a visual tool only. It isn't engineering advice or a check of track capacity, clearances or safety. Verify your plan with qualified people and follow the applicable railway rules before using it.
